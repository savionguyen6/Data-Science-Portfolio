What is Data Science to Me?

Data Science to me is the use of gathered information to complete a conclusion or end goal, but as well ways to collect and figure out more data from the information.

Models of social topics and people are a really big part of our world. Data about people and topics give people knowledge about certain things; it's how you gather more information, how you use the information, and how you use data that is waiting to be used for a certain outcome. However, how the data is being used is a topic of ethics and morals, as there are laws, but not everyone has the mind of the human collective good. For the topic of it being used, the idea of social media comes to mind. When people go on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or any other online platform, you are presented with a For You page, and that is based on data collected about the user. What they watch, how long they spend on it, etc, but it does not mean it's exactly what's happening, as some try to push an agenda, use data, trends, and patterns to showcase many other things, and some may choose to use your data for crude purposes or their own gain. To come to the topic of the responsibility of data collection and usage, it's all about ethics and morals. For a person who is not in such fields, they can only go along with the world; having your data collected and used for other purposes, and if you don't seem to live in this data world, no data is still data. On the other hand, whether the people in charge of handling it are doing right or wrong, we trust in the person and the law to have the power to maintain stability and a moral code. Final send-off note. The knowledge I hope to gain from this course is the power to put data and ideas into solutions, and the ability to use your mind and skills to get the outcome that best serves. Get the skills, get the experience, get the right tools, get a future.
