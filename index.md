# About Me
I'm Savio Nguyen, a Data Science student at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte, learning and developing skills on how to handle and use data to solve real-world problems and questions. Though I can not say I have an interesting background, I can say I have an honest one. I'm a guy who did not know what he wanted to do with his life or his career; I was told most of my life to do this and that, unsure what I wanted. But now I'm here, a data science student, and I will say this: the future is filled with uncertainty.

## My Goals
I could say the basic career goal is, "My goal is to continue developing my data science skills by working on projects that allow me to analyze data, create visualizations, and communicate useful findings, and to gain the knowledge and skills to obtain a job and earn a fulfilling amount of money to sustain myself." Well, the career goal I want is to improve how I approach work, as when it comes to a task or project, I put in effort to make it perfect, as detailed as possible, and sometimes it slows me down; it compels me to feel that it would never be good enough, and it stops me in my tracks. That could be said for a career: take your time; rushing things only causes stress when all I want in this life is to be happy, free. For fulfillment in the work, for fulfillment out of the work.
